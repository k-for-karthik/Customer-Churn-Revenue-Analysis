# Customer Churn And Revenue Analysis

## 🚀 Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis – ClassPass

### 🧠 Business Problem
ClassPass, a fitness subscription platform, faced high customer churn impacting recurring revenue and long-term growth. The business lacked clarity on which subscription plans had the highest churn, how much revenue was lost, and how customer retention affected Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

### 📊 Data & Tools Used
- **Data Size:** 100,000+ subscription records  
- **Tools:** `SQL`, `Power BI`

### 🔍 Approach
1. Cleaned and standardized customer subscription data using SQL (e.g., corrected plan types, null handling).
2. Calculated monthly churn rates and plan-wise churn percentages.
3. Measured revenue loss by subscription type and average CLV.
4. Analyzed average customer lifespan across different plans.
5. Built a Power BI dashboard for dynamic churn monitoring and business reporting.

### 💡 Key Findings
- **Basic Plan** had the highest churn rate (2.75%) but also the **highest CLV** ($110.17).
- **Premium users** churned quicker than expected, reducing expected CLV.
- **Enterprise customers** had the lowest retention despite higher per-user revenue.
- **Churn peaked** in May 2024 (0.14%), while **February** had the lowest revenue.

### 📈 Impact & Outcome
- Identified over **$100K in lost revenue** across subscription plans.
- Informed leadership's **retention campaigns** for Premium and Enterprise users.
- Supported **seasonal marketing strategies** to boost revenue during low-performing months.
- Enabled data-backed decisions via a Power BI dashboard, leading to a **projected 12% reduction in churn**.
